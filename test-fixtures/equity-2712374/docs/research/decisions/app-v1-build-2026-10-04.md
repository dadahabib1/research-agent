### theme-flip-cross-bound-verdict: the theme materiality test checks the verdict the user acts on

```yaml
name: theme-flip-cross-bound-verdict
status: accepted
decided: 2026-10-04
decided_by: requester
moves_valuation: false
depends_on: []
supersedes: []
parameters: []
app: {spec: [ticket 7, ticket 10], merged: ["PR #77"]}
```

- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** With two discount-rate bounds, the theme's flip test must check the cross-bound verdict (HOLD when the bounds disagree), not the verdict at either bound.
- **Parameters:** none
- **Rule:**
  1. Compute the theme's value change at each bound with that bound's own discount rate.
  2. The theme is material when the cross-bound verdict under the theme's bull or bear case differs from the one under the base theme.
  3. The size test runs at each bound with that bound's expected value and margin of safety; material when it passes at any bound.
  4. Per-bound flips stay in the output as a diagnostic, never as the gate.
  5. Once the rate question settles to one bound, the test runs on it alone.
- **Acceptance tests:**
  - Inputs: the RRX case (bound A BUY, bound B HOLD) under theme bear, base and bull → expected output: cross-bound verdict HOLD in all three; not material
  - Inputs: a constructed case with bound B just below the 3-percentage-point minimum alpha, where theme bull lifts bound B to BUY → expected output: the cross-bound verdict changes; material
  - Fixture: none
- **Constraints and non-goals:** a single-bound test misses flips of the reported verdict and reports false ones.
- **Evidence:** `actuator-supply-chain.md` §3.1 (materiality gate); grade not assigned
- **Depends on:** none
- **Supersedes:** none
- **Open items:** none

### fact-store-same-time-conflicts: two different values published at the same moment are a conflict

```yaml
name: fact-store-same-time-conflicts
status: accepted
decided: 2026-10-04
decided_by: requester
moves_valuation: false
depends_on: [known-from-rule]
supersedes: []
parameters: []
app: {spec: [ticket 15], merged: ["PR #55"]}
```

- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** The fact store must keep every distinct value it receives; it must not drop a second, different value silently. Two different values for the same tag, unit, period and dimensions with the same publication time, in one filing or in two filings accepted at the same moment, must be recorded as a conflict and reported, never resolved by picking one.
- **Parameters:** none
- **Rule:** an identical re-delivery adds nothing. A different value under the same identity is stored and a check result names both values and their filings. An as-of query or series read reports the conflict instead of choosing.
- **Acceptance tests:**
  - Inputs: the same fact delivered twice → expected output: one stored fact
  - Inputs: a different value with the same publication time, in one filing or two → expected output: both stored, reported as a conflict
  - Inputs: an amended filing (10-K/A) and a restated comparative in a later filing, queried before and after the later filing's acceptance → expected output: the original before, the restatement after
  - Fixture: none
- **Constraints and non-goals:** amendments and restatements in later filings were already kept as versions; this covers values within one filing or one moment.
- **Evidence:** a code review finding on 2026-10-04; grade not assigned
- **Depends on:** known-from-rule
- **Supersedes:** none
- **Open items:** none

### filing-inputs-from-the-store: the valuation reads balance-sheet, cash-flow and segment inputs from stored filings

```yaml
name: filing-inputs-from-the-store
status: accepted
decided: 2026-10-04
decided_by: requester
moves_valuation: false
depends_on: [fact-store-same-time-conflicts, known-from-rule]
supersedes: ["the hand-entered balance-sheet, share and segment inputs of rrx_reference_case.py, for the app"]
parameters: []
app: {spec: [ticket 11], merged: ["PR #76"]}
```

- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** The figures the RRX valuation took by hand must come from the point-in-time fact store, so a case can be reproduced as of any date:
  - balance-sheet items from one 10-Q date: cash, current and long-term debt, pension and post-retirement liabilities, operating lease liabilities, noncontrolling interests;
  - share counts: outstanding at the balance-sheet and cover-page dates, basic and diluted weighted averages;
  - cash-flow items: depreciation, amortization of acquired intangibles (kept separate), stock-based compensation, capital spending;
  - working capital: receivables, inventories, payables;
  - segment sales and segment profit from each filing's XBRL, and segment adjusted EBITDA from earnings-release exhibits, each kept only with its verified quote or table cell.
- **Parameters:** none
- **Rule:**
  1. **Two depreciation figures, never one for the other.** GAAP depreciation comes from XBRL; the depreciation in the company's adjusted EBITDA reconciliation comes from the earnings release, with its verified quote. For RRX FY2025 they are $154.5M and $153.6M; the reference case uses $153.6M.
  2. **Segment recasts.** When a later filing restates segment history under a new structure, a segment series read as of a date uses one definition throughout: the one in the latest filing known by then. A year with no figure under that definition is reported, never filled from the old one. A recast is recorded as a check result.
  3. **Each release extracted once.** Extracted figures are stored per release, accepted and rejected alike; a stored release is never extracted again.
- **Acceptance tests:**
  - Inputs: RRX as of 2026-09-30 → expected output, at 2026-06-30: cash $441.6M; debt $24.2M + $4,587.6M; pension $99.6M; noncontrolling interests $8.1M; leases $40.7M + $113.9M; PP&E $868.7M; receivables $580.0M; inventories $1,377.9M; payables $642.4M; shares 66.6M outstanding, 66.6M basic and 66.9M diluted weighted average
  - Inputs: RRX FY2025 → expected output: stock-based compensation $37.3M; GAAP depreciation $154.5M; reconciliation depreciation $153.6M with its quote; segment sales and adjusted EBITDA AMC $1,689.8M / $347.9M, IPS $2,594.1M / $686.5M, PES $1,650.6M / $272.7M
  - Inputs: a constructed recast (a later filing restating prior years under renamed or merged segments) → expected output: recast detected; a read after it uses only the new definition, one before it only the old; no series mixes the two
  - Fixture: none
- **Constraints and non-goals:** checks: segments sum to the total, balance-sheet items share one date, known tag changes (such as RRX's 2024 revenue tag) are mapped.
- **Evidence:** RRX's FY2025 10-K and the Q4 2025 earnings release (8-K 0000082811-26-000041, EX-99.1); grade not assigned
- **Depends on:** fact-store-same-time-conflicts, known-from-rule
- **Supersedes:** the hand-entered balance-sheet, share and segment inputs of `rrx_reference_case.py`, for the app only; the script keeps them as its reference values
- **Open items:** the $0.9M gap between the two depreciation figures is not explained by the release

### early-close-known-limitation: early-close days keep the 16:00 close

```yaml
name: early-close-known-limitation
status: accepted
decided: 2026-10-04
decided_by: requester
moves_valuation: false
depends_on: [known-from-rule]
supersedes: []
parameters:
  - {name: market_close, value: "16:00", unit: US Eastern time, applies_on_early_close_days: true}
app: {spec: ["Known limitations", ticket 15], merged: ["PR #55"]}
```

- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** The known-from rule may keep its fixed 16:00 US Eastern close on early-close sessions (13:00 close), as a known limitation. It must be revisited at once if the app ever acts on same-day reactions to news, such as entries on earnings day.
- **Parameters:**
  - `market_close` = 16:00 US Eastern on every session, including early-close days
- **Rule:** unchanged from `known-from-rule`. When a later ticket needs an exchange calendar anyway, early closes come from a maintained calendar package (such as `exchange_calendars`), not hard-coded dates.
- **Acceptance tests:**
  - Inputs: a publication at 14:00 US Eastern on an early-close day → expected output: known that same day (today's behaviour, pinned by a test)
  - Fixture: none
- **Constraints and non-goals:** about three sessions a year, a window of up to three hours; the app decides at most quarterly and scores over months to years, so this cannot move the accuracy metrics.
- **Evidence:** a review finding during ticket 9, 2026-10-04; grade not assigned
- **Depends on:** known-from-rule
- **Supersedes:** none
- **Open items:** none
