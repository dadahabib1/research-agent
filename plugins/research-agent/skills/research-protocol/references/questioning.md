# Questioning protocol

Used at intake (step 2) and in the decision session (step 9). Adapted from the grilling method.

- **Map the open questions as a tree.** Each decision or fact can open further questions.
- **Work in rounds.** Each round asks every question whose prerequisites are already settled. A question that depends on another open question waits for a later round.
- **Number each question and give a recommended answer,** with its reason in one line.
- **Look up facts yourself.** Ask the requester only for decisions and for facts about their own situation.
- **Update the tree after each round.** New answers unlock new questions; drop any question an answer made moot.
- **Stop when no unanswered question remains,** with every branch visited and nothing silently assumed. Summarize the shared understanding, and get the requester's confirmation before acting.
- **Keep it proportionate.** When the gaps are few and independent, one round is enough.
