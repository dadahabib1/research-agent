## 1. How to use this brief

- Read sections 2 to 7 before starting any prompt.
- Settled decisions (section 3) are inputs, not research questions. Propose changing one only with evidence.
- Where a prompt and this brief conflict, follow the prompt for its topic and record the conflict.
